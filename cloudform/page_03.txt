2. Related Work and Design Context


Binary container formats for machine-learning artifacts have proliferated in recent years. Safetensors
emphasizes zero-copy safety and simple tensor maps; GGML/GGUF target quantized inference on commodity
hardware; ONNX and TorchScript focus on computational graphs. None of these formats natively support
ordered progressive refinement or constellation-aware routing. Progressive image and video codecs (JPEG
progressive, progressive mesh transmission, scalable video coding) demonstrate the value of layered delivery,
yet they operate on continuous media rather than discrete knowledge or weight structures.


In the space domain, CCSDS packet telemetry and File Delivery Protocol (CFDP) provide reliable transfer over
deep-space links but treat payloads as opaque byte streams. Cloudform sits at a higher semantic layer: it
assumes an underlying reliable or erasure-coded transport and concentrates on making the payload itself
progressively useful and GPU-ready. The closest conceptual relatives are progressive mesh streaming in
computer graphics and bit-plane coding in successive-approximation A/D conversion; Cloudform adapts these
ideas to the specific needs of quantized neural and knowledge artifacts.

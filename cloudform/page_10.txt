9. Discussion, Limitations, and Future Directions


Cloudform’s progressive bit-plane model assumes that a meaningful significance ordering exists over the
payload. For many weight tensors and edge-weighted graphs this ordering is natural; for unstructured opaque
blobs the progressive benefit collapses to ordinary sequential streaming. Future work should explore learned or
task-specific significance orderings that maximize early utility for particular downstream inference tasks.


The current design deliberately omits an embedded computational graph; Cloudform is an artifact container, not
a full model-execution format. Composition with ONNX, TorchScript, or custom rad-hard runtimes is left to the
surrounding system. Extending the header with optional lightweight execution hints is a natural evolution path.


Security considerations include the strength of the chosen hash and signature algorithms under the radiation
and side-channel environment of LEO, as well as the implications of allowing partial artifacts to drive
autonomous decisions. These topics warrant dedicated follow-on study.

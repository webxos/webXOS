7. LLM-Native Design and Inter-Model Transfer


A distinctive requirement of Cloudform is that the header be consumable and generable by large language
models. The compact key-value binary encoding is accompanied by a short natural-language LLM seed that
describes the semantic content and intended use of the megalith. This seed enables an upstream model to emit
a complete, valid Cloudform package (“vibe-code a 1-bit sensor archive for anomaly detection”) and enables a
downstream model to understand the artifact without external schema registries.


For LLM-to-LLM pipelines the same progressive mechanism applies: an agent that must transfer a large
specialized expert or a long context fragment can begin useful work on the receiving side after the first planes
arrive, while the remainder of the transfer proceeds asynchronously. The deterministic binary layout further
allows both models to treat the artifact as a first-class, hash-addressable object rather than a transient byte
stream.

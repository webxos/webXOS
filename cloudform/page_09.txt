8. System Integration: Satellite, Cloud, and Earth


On the satellite side, flight software (or an on-orbit LLM agent) produces Cloudform packages either from raw
sensor streams or from inference outputs of radiation-hardened TPUs. The progressive emission order is
chosen according to the expected remaining contact time and the priority of the scientific or operational task.
Ground stations or optical relay nodes receive planes, verify the hash chain, and may forward partial artifacts
onward to cloud regions for immediate low-fidelity analysis.


In the terrestrial cloud the same .cfb file can be memory-mapped and the buffers handed directly to GPU
kernels with minimal or zero transformation, satisfying the GPU_ALIGNED contract. When higher fidelity is later
required, additional planes are requested or retrieved from cold storage and the reconstruction is refined in
place. This staged refinement path is especially attractive for cost-sensitive inference pipelines that can operate
on a coarse model for the majority of queries and escalate only when confidence thresholds are not met.
